Questions

Honest answers.

What's the catch with 3 months free?

No catch. We're early stage and learning from our first customers. Free access in exchange for honest feedback. You can walk away at any point with all your data, no questions asked.

What happens after 3 months?

We talk pricing together based on your fleet size and what you actually used. Our goal is a price that's a small fraction of what one missed repair costs you. If we can't agree on a fair number, you keep your data and there's no obligation.

How big is your team?

Small and local to the UAE. That's the whole point — when you message us about a problem, you reach the people who built the product, not a support queue.

Do you work with very small fleets (under 10 vehicles)?

Yes. That's actually who Waei is built for. Most fleet software has a high minimum that locks out fleets under 50 vehicles. We're the opposite.

Does it work in Arabic?

The interface is currently English, but our invoice scanning understands Arabic invoices end-to-end. Arabic UI is on the roadmap and pilot customers help us prioritize it.

Where is my data stored?

On Supabase infrastructure (EU region by default). Encrypted in transit and at rest. We can discuss specific data residency requirements during onboarding.

Can I see the product before committing to a call?

Yes — click "Try the app" to see the live product. You can poke around the login flow. For real data and a tour, the 30-minute call is much more useful.

Ready to start?

One email, one call, and you're set up. Three months to decide if Waei is worth paying for.
